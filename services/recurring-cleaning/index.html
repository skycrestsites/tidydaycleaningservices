Overview

Why Austin clients choose our recurring cleaning

Recurring cleaning is the easiest way to keep your home feeling calm and cared for. Choose weekly, biweekly or monthly visits and we will show up on your schedule with the same checklist and the same attention to detail every time.

Because we visit regularly, we get to know your home and how you like things done. Little preferences, like how you want the throw pillows arranged or which rooms to focus on, become part of the routine.

Ideal for

  • Families with kids and pets
  • Busy professionals
  • Anyone who wants to stop thinking about cleaning

Questions

Recurring Cleaning FAQ

Do not see your question? Send us a message and we will get right back to you.

Which frequency should I choose?

Weekly suits busy households with kids or pets, biweekly is our most popular choice, and monthly works well for smaller homes or tidy households that want a regular reset.

Do I need a deep clean first?

If your home has not been professionally cleaned recently, we usually start with a deep clean so your recurring visits can focus on maintaining that standard.

Can I skip or move a visit?

Of course. Just let us know ahead of time and we will find a new time that works for you.
