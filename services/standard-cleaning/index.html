Overview

Why Austin clients choose our standard house cleaning

Life in Austin moves fast. Between work, school runs and everything else, keeping up with the dusting and the bathrooms is usually the first thing to slip. Our standard house cleaning takes that weight off your shoulders with a thorough, consistent clean of every main living space.

We follow a room by room checklist so nothing gets skipped, and we treat your home with the same care we would want in our own. Expect clean surfaces, fresh bathrooms, a sparkling kitchen and floors that feel good under bare feet.

Ideal for

  • Busy professionals and families
  • Homes that are already in good shape
  • Anyone who wants a reliable reset each visit

Checklist

What is included

Every standard house cleaning follows this checklist. Have something specific in mind? Just let us know when you request your quote.

  • Dusting of reachable surfaces, shelves and décor
  • Kitchen counters, backsplash and sink cleaned and shined
  • Exterior of appliances and microwave inside wiped
  • Bathrooms: toilets, tubs, showers, sinks and mirrors
  • Vacuuming of carpets and rugs
  • Mopping of hard floors
  • Beds made and linens changed when left out
  • Trash emptied and liners replaced
  • Light tidying and straightening throughout
  • Light switches, door handles and high touch areas wiped

Questions

Standard House Cleaning FAQ

Do not see your question? Send us a message and we will get right back to you.

How long does a standard clean take?

Most homes take two to four hours depending on size, the number of bathrooms and the current condition. We will give you a time estimate with your quote.

Do I need to be home during the clean?

Not at all. Many clients give us access and come home to a finished clean. We are happy to work around whatever is most comfortable for you.

What if my home needs more than a standard clean?

If it has been a while since your last professional clean, we usually recommend starting with a deep clean, then moving to standard visits to keep things fresh.
