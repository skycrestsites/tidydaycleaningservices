Meet Tidy Day

Cleaning with a personal touch

Tidy Day Cleaning Services was founded with a simple idea: a clean home should feel calm, cared for and completely yours. We are a local Austin company that believes the little details are what make a clean feel truly finished.

Every visit follows a thoughtful checklist, and every client gets the kind of friendly, respectful service we would want in our own homes. Whether you need a one time reset or a regular helping hand, we are here to make your days a little lighter.

Ask Us Anything
What areas do you serve?

We serve Austin, TX and the surrounding communities, including Round Rock, Cedar Park, Pflugerville, Georgetown, Leander, Lakeway, Bee Cave, Buda, Kyle and nearby areas. If you are close by, just ask.

How do I get a price?

Send us a quick message through the quote form with your home size, the service you are interested in and your preferred timing. We will follow up with a personalized quote.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes. We arrive with our own supplies and equipment. If you prefer specific or fragrance free products, just let us know and we are happy to use them.

Do I need to be home?

Not at all. Many clients provide access and come home to a finished clean. Whatever feels most comfortable to you works for us.

What if something was missed?

Your happiness matters to us. If anything is not quite right, reach out and we will make it right.
